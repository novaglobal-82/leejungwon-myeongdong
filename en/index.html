MYEONGDONG · EULJIRO

3,000 cuts of care,
in every single bite

Between Myeongdong and Euljiro — from aged pork belly scored 3,000 times to 1++ Hanwoo beef. Not just a meal, but an experience.

01 · SIGNATURE

Aged pork belly,
scored 3,000 times

Our signature is aged pork belly from carefully selected Korean sows, scored 3,000 times. The fine cuts make it crispy outside and juicy inside from the very first bite. Our aged premium pork neck, whole galmaegisal with a beef-like aroma, and chewy kkodeulsal bring out the true charm of good pork.

02 · PREMIUM HANWOO

1++ Hanwoo,
for special occasions

For special occasions, try our 1++ grade Hanwoo beef. Along with melt-in-your-mouth ribeye and tenderloin and chewy rib finger and flat iron, we offer rare cuts like ribeye cap and chuck flap in limited quantities. Set menus let you enjoy several cuts at once.

03 · MEALS & GATHERING

From hearty meals
to group dinners

Our meal menu — soft tofu stew made with a recipe passed down from Hwasim-ri's original tofu alley, Gwangju-style spicy zucchini stew, blue crab doenjang stew and sweet-and-sour braised kimchi — offers hearty, reasonably priced meals loved by nearby office workers. Our spacious dining room is perfect for team dinners and gatherings. Group reservations are welcome; please contact us in advance.
